## 🎯 **Best Results Tips**

### For Your Voice Sample:
- Record in quiet environment
- Speak clearly and naturally
- Include varied intonation
- 16kHz or 22kHz sample rate

### For Telugu Text:
- Use proper Telugu Unicode
- Avoid mixing scripts
- Break long sentences
- Test with simple phrases first
